# Brand Guidelines — Mechatronopolis

## Brand Position

Mechatronopolis represents practical, future-forward robotics and mechatronics execution.

## Voice

- Clear
- Technical
- Outcome-focused

## Messaging Pillars

1. Build real systems, not just concepts.
2. Keep execution measurable and deployable.
3. Ship with market and operations readiness in mind.

## Visual Direction (Baseline)

- Clean technical layouts
- High-contrast, modern engineering aesthetic
- Documentation-first presentation for trust and traceability
